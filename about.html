About Fixed Assets Bookkeeping

Straightforward books, handled by one person you actually know.

No account managers, no rotating staff. Just accurate, on-time bookkeeping from someone who picks up the phone.

The bookkeeper

Arnika Addison

Arnika founded Fixed Assets Bookkeeping in Montevallo, Alabama to give small businesses what larger firms often can't: direct, personal attention to their books every single month. Every client's ledger, payroll, and tax filings pass through her hands — not a rotating team of assistants.

Her approach is simple: reconcile everything, categorize it correctly, and keep the numbers current so tax season is a formality rather than a scramble. Clients range from solo contractors to multi-employee small businesses across a range of industries.
