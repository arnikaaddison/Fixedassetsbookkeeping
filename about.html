About Fixed Assets Bookkeeping

Straightforward books, handled by one person you actually know.

No account managers, no rotating staff. Just accurate, on-time bookkeeping from someone who picks up the phone.

Get to know

Arnika Addison

I didn't start this business just to prepare tax returns — I started it to help people breathe again when it comes to their finances.

Over the years, I've worked with clients who were afraid to open IRS letters, unsure where their money was going, or overwhelmed by years of unfiled taxes and disorganized books. Many felt ashamed or stuck. I knew there had to be a better way to support people through those moments — with professionalism, education, and zero judgment.

Why This Work Matters to Me

I founded my bookkeeping and tax practice with one clear mission: to help individuals and business owners gain clarity, confidence, and control over their finances. Too many people feel overwhelmed, embarrassed, or stuck when it comes to their books or their taxes — especially after years of unfiled returns, messy records, or unexpected IRS notices. I've seen firsthand how quickly financial stress can impact families, businesses, and long-term opportunities. My goal is to remove that stress by providing accurate bookkeeping, strategic tax preparation, and practical guidance rooted in compliance and transparency.

Who I Work With

I specialize in working with small business owners, self-employed individuals, and taxpayers who need more than just "tax filing." My services focus on building clean, audit-ready books, maximizing legitimate deductions, resolving tax issues, and helping clients plan — not panic — when it comes to taxes.

My Approach

My approach combines accurate bookkeeping, compliant tax preparation, and proactive tax strategy. I help clients understand their numbers, stay organized year-round, and make informed decisions that protect both their income and their peace of mind.

This work matters to me because I believe financial organization is a form of empowerment. When your numbers are right, you make better decisions, protect what you're building, and move forward with confidence. My "why" is simple: everyone deserves access to clear financial guidance and a fresh start. I take pride in helping my clients move from confusion to confidence — one clean report and one completed return at a time.
