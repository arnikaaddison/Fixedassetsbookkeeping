The problem

Your Client Is Ready to File. Their Books Aren't.

Unclassified transactions

Months of activity sitting uncategorized, with no clear picture of income or expenses.

Personal and business mixed together

One account, two kinds of spending, and hours spent sorting one from the other.

Missing or incomplete records

Bank statements, receipts, and invoices scattered rather than organized.

No accurate P&L

No reliable profit and loss statement to actually build the return from.

Unreconciled books

Numbers that don't tie out to the bank, with no way to know what's real.

A full year to reconstruct

An entire year of financial activity that has to be rebuilt before the return can even start.

Before you can prepare the return, you're spending valuable time trying to clean up or reconstruct the books. That's where FAB comes in. Let us handle the bookkeeping so you can focus on what you do best — preparing accurate tax returns and serving your tax clients.
