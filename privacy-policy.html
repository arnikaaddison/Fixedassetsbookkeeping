Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Fixed Assets Bookkeeping & Tax Solutions ("FAB," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through fixedassetsbookkeeping.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit a contact or consultation request form
  • Sign up for a lead magnet, checklist, or email list
  • Apply to the FAB Partner Network or submit a partner referral
  • Email or call us directly

This may include your name, business name, email address, phone number, and any details you choose to share about your bookkeeping or tax situation. We do not collect Social Security numbers, banking information, or sensitive tax documents through general website forms.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, evaluate partner applications and referrals, send requested resources or updates, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The Site uses third-party tools to operate certain features, including form and application processing (Jotform) and, where applicable, analytics tools to understand how the Site is used. These providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party service linked from this Site.

Client Engagements

If you become a bookkeeping or tax client, financial and tax information you provide as part of that engagement is handled according to the confidentiality and professional standards described in your service agreement, separate from general website form submissions, and is not stored on this website.

Cookies

The Site may use basic cookies or similar technologies to support normal website functionality. We do not currently use cookies for third-party advertising.

Your Choices

You may request that we delete information you've submitted through a website form, or unsubscribe from any email list, at any time by contacting us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
